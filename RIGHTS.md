# Rights and attribution

No repository-wide reuse license is assigned here. File-specific upstream notices and the terms of the original providers remain applicable. The source overlay requires the separately hosted BEVFormerFusion base checkout.

The replay scripts obtain the [FHWA NGSIM dataset](https://doi.org/10.21949/1504477) and the [JRC OpenACC dataset](https://doi.org/10.2905/JRC.KMH3D00) directly from their providers. The repository contains aggregate replay results, not copies of their raw trajectories. The historical Layer-Gate publications are linked through the [existing research release](https://github.com/xiaotwu/layergate-bev-critical-miss-evaluation), which has its own data and rights notices.

The PDFs are research manuscripts formatted with [IEEEtran](https://ctan.org/pkg/ieeetran). They make no claim of IEEE publication, review, or endorsement.
