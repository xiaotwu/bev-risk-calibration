# Supporting Results

[Manuscript PDF](observation-age-stopping-support-paper.pdf) · [Horizon-specific model scores](horizon_baseline.csv)

The score table reports the comparison of shared and separately fitted horizon models for both stopping-support targets at the day, campaign, and campaign-average levels. The manuscript describes the grouped evaluation and calibration. See the [JRC OpenACC dataset](https://doi.org/10.2905/JRC.KMH3D00), [nuScenes](https://www.nuscenes.org/), and the [archived Layer-Gate comparison](https://github.com/xiaotwu/layergate-bev-critical-miss-evaluation/releases/tag/v1.1.0) for data and detector provenance.

The [reproducibility checks](../../README.md#reproducibility) verify the manuscript PDF and the archived simulation and replay evidence. Repeating the trajectory fits and detector replay requires the corresponding project inputs and runtime.
